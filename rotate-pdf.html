Rotate PDF Online

FileSwift Tools Rotate PDF lets you rotate your PDF document pages by 90°, 180° or 270°. The processing happens directly in your browser.

Features

  • Rotate PDF pages by 90°, 180° or 270°.
  • Works with multi-page PDF documents.
  • Preview the PDF before downloading.
  • No account or registration required.
  • Mobile-friendly design.
  • Browser-based PDF processing.

How to Rotate a PDF

1

Select

Select the PDF file from your phone, tablet or computer.

2

Rotate

Choose 90°, 180° or 270° and click Rotate PDF.

3

Download

Your rotated PDF will be downloaded automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Rotate PDF tool free?

Yes. FileSwift Tools provides this PDF rotation tool free of charge.

Can I rotate a multi-page PDF?

Yes. The selected rotation is applied to every page of the PDF.

Which rotation angles are available?

You can rotate the PDF by 90°, 180° or 270°.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

The PDF is processed directly in your browser. It does not need to be uploaded to a conversion server.

Can I use this tool on mobile?

Yes. The tool is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop browsers.

What happens to my original PDF?

Your original file is not modified. A new rotated PDF file is created for download.
