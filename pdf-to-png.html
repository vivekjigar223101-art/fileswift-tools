Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF to PNG converter free?

Yes. FileSwift Tools provides this PDF to PNG converter free of charge.

Are my PDF files uploaded to a server?

The conversion is performed directly in your browser using client-side processing. The PDF does not need to be uploaded to a conversion server.

Can I convert a multi-page PDF?

Yes. Every page of the selected PDF can be converted into a separate PNG image.

Can I download all PNG images together?

Yes. When multiple pages are converted, you can download all PNG files together in a ZIP archive.

Can I use this tool on mobile?

Yes. The converter is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and desktop browsers.

What happens with password-protected PDFs?

Password-protected or encrypted PDFs may require a password and may not be supported by this tool.
