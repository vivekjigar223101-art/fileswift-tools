Free Online PDF Splitter

FileSwift Tools allows you to extract pages from PDF documents. Select the pages you need, split every page into a separate PDF, or download selected pages individually.

How to split a PDF?

  1. Upload your PDF document.
  2. Choose your preferred splitting method.
  3. Enter page numbers if required.
  4. Preview your PDF pages.
  5. Tap Split PDF.
  6. Download your new PDF files.

Is this PDF splitter free?

Yes. You can split PDF documents for free without registration.

Are my files uploaded?

No. PDF processing happens locally in your browser. Your selected document is not sent to a FileSwift server.
