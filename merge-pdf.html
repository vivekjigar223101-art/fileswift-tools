Free Online PDF Merger

FileSwift Tools allows you to combine multiple PDF documents into one file. Upload your documents, arrange their order, and create a single merged PDF directly in your browser.

How to merge PDF files?

  1. Tap Choose PDF Files.
  2. Select two or more PDF documents.
  3. Use the arrow buttons to arrange their order.
  4. Remove any unwanted document.
  5. Tap Merge PDF Files.
  6. Download your merged PDF.

Is this PDF merger free?

Yes. You can combine PDF files for free without creating an account.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. PDF merging is performed locally in your browser. Your selected documents are not sent to a FileSwift server.
