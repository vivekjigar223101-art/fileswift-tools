How to Compress PDF Online?

1. Select a PDF file from your device.

2. Choose High Quality, Balanced or Small Size.

3. Click the Compress PDF button.

4. Wait for the conversion to finish.

5. Check the new file size and download your PDF.

Is this PDF Compressor Free?

Yes. FileSwift Tools provides this browser-based PDF compression tool without requiring an account or payment.

Are My PDF Files Uploaded?

Your selected PDF is processed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded to a FileSwift server. Required processing libraries are loaded from third-party CDNs.
